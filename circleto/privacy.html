Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Circleto app ("Circleto", "we", "us") collects, why, and what you can do about it. Circleto is run by Malothu Hemanth, an individual developer based in India. Questions: circleto.help@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, your password (stored only in scrambled, "hashed" form; we never see it) and the first name you choose.
  • Your challenges: which challenges you start, your daily Yes/No answers and their dates, your streaks and badges.
  • Groups: the private groups you create or join, and invite codes.
  • Photos (optional): photos you choose to add to a check-in.
  • Private notes (optional): short notes you add to a check-in.
  • Your own challenges (Premium): the name, rules and settings of challenges you create.
  • Safety: people you block and photos you report.
  • Interest in Premium: if you tap "Notify me" on the Premium screen, we record that you tapped it.

Some things stay only on your phone and are never sent to us: your appearance setting, your reminder time, photos you hid after reporting them, and the guess of your region (from your phone's time zone and language) that we use to show local food examples.

We do not collect your location, contacts or health data, we do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or tracking tools.

Who can see what

  • Only you: your email, your private notes and your solo challenges.
  • People in your groups: your first name, whether you checked in each day in that group's challenge, your progress in it, and any photos you add to that challenge's check-ins.
  • Nobody else. There is no public profile or public feed. People you block cannot see your activity, and you cannot see theirs.

Why we use your information

  • To run the app: your account, challenges, groups, streaks, badges and reminders.
  • To keep people safe: blocking, and reviewing reported photos.
  • To improve Circleto, for example by counting how many people use a feature.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Services we use

Your account and app data (including photos) are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, which keeps photos in private storage that is not publicly reachable. App downloads go through the app stores (for example Google Play), which have their own privacy policies. When paid Premium launches, purchases will be handled by the app store and a subscription service; we will update this policy before that happens. We never see or store your card details.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, challenges, check-ins, notes, photos and group memberships. Reports you made about others may be kept for safety reasons, without your name attached.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account any time: Settings → Delete account. This cannot be undone.
  • Change your name in Settings, and remove photos or notes from your check-ins.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it: email circleto.help@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

Age

Circleto is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

We use encrypted connections, private storage for photos, and database rules so that people can only see what this policy describes. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

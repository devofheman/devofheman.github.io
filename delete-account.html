Delete your Circleto account

Circleto is made by CogitatingDevofheman (Malothu Hemanth).

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Circleto and sign in.
  2. Go to You → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away. This cannot be undone.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Email circleto.help@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Circleto account", from the email address you signed up with. We will delete your account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (email, name) and sign-in details
  • Your challenges, daily check-ins, streaks, badges and private notes
  • Your group memberships (if you host a group, hosting passes to another member)
  • Your blocked list and Premium interest

What may be kept

Reports you sent about other people may be kept for safety reasons, without your name attached. Nothing else is kept after deletion.
